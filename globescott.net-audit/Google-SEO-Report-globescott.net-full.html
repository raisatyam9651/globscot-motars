2. Audit Categories

2.1 Technical SEO

Score: 35/100

What Works

  • Clean, keyword-friendly extensionless URLs with trailing slashes enforced via .htaccess
  • 301 redirect from .php to canonical directory URL
  • Valid viewport meta and lang=en-US declared
  • Semantic breadcrumb navigation with aria-label on interior pages
  • Each page owns a single <main> (no duplicate landmarks from header/footer)

Findings

Relative asset & link paths break on trailing-slash URLs Critical

header.php loads CSS/JS as wp-content/... and wp-includes/... (no leading slash); nav uses href="index/", href="about-us/"; contact form uses action="contact-us/"; some backgrounds use url(wp-content/...). .htaccess 301-redirects every page to a directory URL (e.g. /about-us/). A browser resolves wp-content/themes/.../style.css on /about-us/ to /about-us/wp-content/themes/.../style.css, which does not exist -> the page renders unstyled and navigation is broken. The homepage linked as index/ has the same failure.

Recommendation: Convert every asset src/href and internal link to root-relative paths (leading /), or add <base href="https://www.globescott.net/"> to <head>. Root-relative is preferred. Verify on the live server after deploy.

No canonical tags site-wide Critical

0 of 21 pages emit <link rel="canonical">. With .php and directory URL variants both resolvable, duplicate-URL indexation risk is high.

Recommendation: Emit a self-referencing absolute canonical in header.php built from SITE_URL + current page slug with trailing slash.

No robots.txt and no XML sitemap High

Neither /robots.txt nor /sitemap.xml exists. Crawlers have no sitemap hint and no crawl directives.

Recommendation: Add robots.txt referencing an XML sitemap listing all 21 canonical URLs (templates in fixes/).

config SITE_URL points to http://localhost:8000 High

includes/config.php defines SITE_URL as http://localhost:8000. Any absolute URL generation (canonical, sitemap, OG, schema) built from this will be wrong in production.

Recommendation: Set SITE_URL to https://www.globescott.net and standardize on the www + https host (matches the absolute image URLs already in the code).

Non-functional WordPress leftover forms Medium

Blog comment forms POST to https://www.globescott.net/wp-comments-post/ and sidebar search forms GET https://www.globescott.net/ — both are WordPress endpoints that no longer exist in the static rebuild, so they 404 / do nothing.

Recommendation: Remove the comment and search widgets, or wire them to a working handler. Dead interactive elements harm UX and crawl budget.

Broken internal link /dc-mo/ Medium

products-pmdc-motors.php links 'DC motor' to href="/dc-mo/", a truncated slug that 404s.

Recommendation: Point it to /products-pmdc-motors/ or /what-is-bldc-motor/ as appropriate.

Render-blocking resources in <head> Medium

~15 separate stylesheets plus jQuery load in <head> unminified/unbundled. No preconnect, no critical-CSS inlining beyond theme inline block.

Recommendation: Bundle & minify CSS, defer non-critical CSS, move jQuery to footer with defer, add preconnect for fonts.

2.2 On-Page SEO

Score: 45/100

What Works

  • Every page has a unique <title>
  • Consistent brand suffix pattern in titles
  • Interior pages use a single H1
  • Descriptive image alt text on product thumbnails and contact icons

Findings

No meta descriptions on any page High

0 of 21 pages define a meta description; header.php never outputs one. Google will auto-generate snippets, reducing CTR control.

Recommendation: Add a $meta_description variable per page and output <meta name="description"> in header.php. 140-160 chars, keyword + value proposition + location.

Homepage has 4 H1 tags Medium

The homepage slider renders 4 <h1> tags (Driving Motorized Innovation, Guaranteed Faster Engineering Solution, Best Motors For Amuzement Park Rides, Motors For Material Handeling Equipments). Multiple H1s dilute topical focus and none contains the primary keyword 'DC motor manufacturer'.

Recommendation: Keep one H1 (e.g. 'DC, BLDC & Geared Motor Manufacturer in India') and demote slider slide headings to <h2>/<span>.

Titles under-optimized (no keywords/location, brand-first, all-caps) Medium

Titles like 'Products – GLOBE SCOTT MOTORS PVT LTD' and 'About us – ...' lead with generic labels and all-caps brand; product/blog titles omit high-intent keywords and location (India / Gujarat).

Recommendation: Front-load keywords: e.g. 'PMDC Motors Manufacturer & Supplier in India | Globe Scott Motors'. Use title case, keep 50-60 chars.

Spelling errors baked into URLs, titles and H1s Medium

URL/title/H1 misspellings: 'Benifits' (benifits-of-using-dc-motors), 'Transaxel' (should be Transaxle; also inconsistent with menu 'PMDC Transaxle'), 'Amuzement', 'Handeling', 'morden'. These appear in indexable slugs and headings.

Recommendation: Correct spellings; 301-redirect old slugs to corrected ones (benefits-of-using-dc-motors, products-pmdc-transaxle).

Missing alt text on key images Low

13 homepage images, 2 about-us, 2 applications images lack alt; the footer logo uses alt="".

Recommendation: Add descriptive alt to all meaningful images; give the footer logo alt="Globe Scott Motors".

2.3 Content Quality

Score: 55/100

What Works

  • Genuine manufacturer expertise: detailed railway-signalling (~1,700 words) and PMDC (~1,000 words) pages
  • Company history since 1985 and Indo-US collaboration story (E-E-A-T)
  • ISO 9001:2015 and CE certification signals on About page
  • Real NAP, phone, email and embedded Google Map

Findings

Thin product & hub pages High

products-bldc-motors (~36 words), applications (~45 words of text over 9 images), products hub (~136 words), products-pmdc-transaxel (~179 words), products-pmdc-geared-motors (~223 words). Too thin to rank for product queries.

Recommendation: Expand each product page to 400-700 words: specs (voltage, power, RPM, torque), applications, materials, differentiators, and a spec table.

Outdated 2020 COVID-19 banner in global header Medium

header.php hardcodes a 'COVID-19 Alert: We have resumed manufacturing...' banner on every page, signalling stale, unmaintained content.

Recommendation: Remove the banner.

No author, dates, or E-E-A-T bylines on blog articles Medium

Blog posts lack visible author, credentials, and publish/updated dates in a structured way; comment sections are non-functional.

Recommendation: Add author bio + engineering credentials, publish/updated dates, and Article schema.

Duplicated boilerplate across product blurbs Low

Product hub blurbs are generic marketing sentences with little unique, specification-rich content.

Recommendation: Replace with unique, spec-driven copy per product family.

2.4 Schema / Structured Data

Score: 5/100

Findings

No structured data anywhere High

0 JSON-LD blocks. Missing Organization, LocalBusiness (the business is a physical manufacturer with NAP + map), Product, BreadcrumbList, Article, and WebSite schema. This blocks rich results and weakens entity/AI understanding.

Recommendation: Add Organization + LocalBusiness (with geo, address, sameAs socials) sitewide, BreadcrumbList on interior pages, Product/ItemList on product pages, and Article on blog posts. Templates in fixes/schema-organization-localbusiness.jsonld.

2.5 Performance (CWV)

Score: 45/100

What Works

  • loading="lazy" and srcset present on several product images
  • Most JavaScript loads before </body> rather than in <head>

Findings

No modern image formats; heavy PNG/JPEG payload High

~10 MB of images across 188 files, 0 WebP/AVIF. Largest single images 300-430 KB; many product photos shipped as PNG. Hurts LCP on image-led hero/slider and product pages.

Recommendation: Convert to WebP/AVIF, compress, and serve appropriately sized variants. Target hero/LCP image < 150 KB.

Heavy homepage slider + many third-party libraries Medium

Homepage uses a Slick slider with large full-bleed backgrounds plus Bootstrap, Isotope, Magnific Popup, WOW, Waypoints, CounterUp, Meanmenu, Popper, jQuery — a large JS footprint for a brochure site.

Recommendation: Remove unused libraries, defer/async scripts, lazy-init the slider, and preload the first slide image.

Unminified/unbundled CSS & JS Medium

~15 CSS and ~17 JS files load individually; several are theme/plugin defaults likely partly unused.

Recommendation: Bundle, minify, tree-shake unused CSS, and add long-cache headers.

2.6 AI Search Readiness (GEO)

Score: 40/100

What Works

  • Clear, extractable factual text on what products are and where the company operates
  • Consistent NAP and company facts

Findings

No entity/structured signals for AI engines Medium

No Organization/LocalBusiness schema, no sameAs to social profiles, no author entities, no FAQ schema. AI Overviews/ChatGPT/Perplexity have weak grounding for brand facts.

Recommendation: Add schema + sameAs, add concise FAQ blocks with FAQPage schema on product/blog pages, and ensure key facts (founded 1985, Indo-US JV, certifications, product families) appear as clean prose.

No llms.txt (optional) Low

No /llms.txt. Optional and ignored by Google, but cheap to add for AI-crawler guidance.

Recommendation: Optionally add /llms.txt summarizing the company and key URLs.

2.7 Local SEO

Score: 50/100

What Works

  • Consistent NAP in header, footer and contact page
  • Embedded Google Map for the Killa Pardi, Gujarat location
  • Linked Facebook, Twitter/X and LinkedIn profiles

Findings

No LocalBusiness schema / geo coordinates Medium

The business is a physical manufacturer but exposes no LocalBusiness/Organization schema with address, geo, openingHours, or sameAs.

Recommendation: Add LocalBusiness JSON-LD (fixes/) and ensure the Google Business Profile is claimed and NAP-consistent.

Map iframe has pointer-events disabled Low

The contact-page map iframe sets pointer-events:none, reducing interactivity.

Recommendation: Allow interaction or provide a 'Get directions' link to the GBP listing.
